Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026. Applies to this website and any project work carried out through it.

1. Who this covers
This policy applies to visitors of this website and to clients engaging K (operated by an individual, referred to here as "I", "me", or "K") for design and development work. This site operates from South Africa and this policy is written to comply with South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

2. What I collect
When you use the contact form, I collect the name, email address, and project details you choose to submit. I don't use cookies, analytics trackers, or any third-party advertising scripts on this site beyond what's needed to serve the page itself.

3. How I use it
Information submitted through the contact form is used only to respond to your enquiry and, if you become a client, to deliver the agreed project. I don't sell, rent, or share your information with third parties, and I don't use it for marketing without your separate consent.

4. How it's stored
Enquiries sent through the contact form are delivered directly to my personal email and handled the same way any other personal correspondence is. I keep client information only as long as reasonably needed to complete the project and meet any legal or accounting requirements after that.

5. Your rights
Under POPIA, you have the right to ask what personal information I hold about you, request corrections, or request it be deleted, unless I'm legally required to keep it (for example, for tax records). To do any of this, email izeerb@gmail.com.

6. Third parties
This site is hosted on GitHub Pages and Vercel, and the contact form uses your device's own email client to send messages — no third-party form-processing service holds your data.

7. Changes to this policy
This policy may be updated occasionally to reflect changes to how the site or business works. The date at the top of this page will always show the latest revision.

8. Contact
Questions about this policy can be sent to izeerb@gmail.com.
